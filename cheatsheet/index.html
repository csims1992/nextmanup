Early-injury prop refunds

This is not a pick. Rules vary by book, state, and market, and already-decided results usually stand. Check your book's current policy before you bet, and look for the eligibility mark on the slip.

  • DraftKings Early Exit Protected. On eligible full-game NFL player props, if the player leaves because of injury in the first half and does not return, the selection can be settled as void. Singles: the stake is returned. In parlays and Same Game Parlays, that leg is voided and the remaining legs are recalculated. Period-specific props are not covered. DraftKings Early Exit Protected policy. DraftKings applied this to qualifying Jaylen Waddle props in Week 9 after he left in the first half and was ruled out; see the Week 9 Waddle write-up.
  • BetMGM BetDefense. On eligible player props tied to positive performance (for example, an over), if the player leaves because of injury in the first quarter, is officially ruled out, and does not return, the stake can be refunded on a straight bet (within 72 hours of settlement). Parlay and Same Game Parlay legs are removed and the rest is recalculated. Unders, weekly specials, and already-settled results are not covered. Not available in every state. Look for the BetDefense icon. No opt-in is required. BetMGM BetDefense policy.

Other books have their own rules. If you can't confirm a policy page, don't assume a refund. Both policy pages above were rechecked Sunday morning and left as written; no new one-line change was needed.
