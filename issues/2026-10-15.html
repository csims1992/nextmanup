If you want exposure to a ceiling game against backup corners, DraftKings' alt ladder shows 99.5+ at +112 and 109.5+ at +155 (LineTerminal). If Moss is ruled out at inactives (about 6:45 PM ET), the matchup gets thinner still.
  • DEN S Talanoa Hufanga: OUT (ankle). ESPN reported he could miss 2–3 weeks (via Broncos Wire). Next man up: Devon Key, the likely starter next to Brandon Jones. Seattle ranks 30th in rush EPA/play (VSiN), and Wilson has gone over his rushing-yards line in only 1 of 4 games (LineTerminal). That makes rush attempts the cleaner volume bet. Holani's receptions/receiving yards are the passing-down angle; he had 24 receiving yards last week (LineTerminal game log).
